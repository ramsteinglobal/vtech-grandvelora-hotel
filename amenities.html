01 / Stillness

The Azure Cistern

Modeled after the ancient stepwells of Amber, The Azure Cistern is a subterranean stone sanctuary shielded from the midday heat. Hand-chiseled quartz steps descend into temperature-tempered natural spring water, illuminated at twilight by ninety floating oil lamps.

Hours

06:00 – 22:00 Daily

Water Temp

28°C Heated Springwater

Signature Element

Twilight Candle Ritual

Depth

Graduated 1.2m to 1.6m
