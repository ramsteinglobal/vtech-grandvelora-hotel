The Philosophy
"True luxury is not ornament. It is the absence of noise, the scent of jasmine carried on evening air, and stone that remembers the sun."

Carved from amber sandstone in 1962 and tenderly preserved across three generations, The Grand Velora stands as an intimate retreat away from the cadence of the modern world. Here, quiet mornings begin with birdsong over the lily pool, and twilights settle under hand-cast bronze lanterns.
